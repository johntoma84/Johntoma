About

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Agriculture from Ain Shams University — Quality Management Department (2024, Grade A) — and I work as a Quality Control Engineer in the food industry. My day-to-day is inspections: warehouses, chillers and freezers, incoming raw materials from suppliers, personal hygiene practices on the floor, and the Salad and Sandwich preparation sections where SOPs matter most. I identify non-conformities, drive corrective and preventive actions, and document everything in clear inspection reports. I also have hands-on experience with bakery products of all kinds — breads, pastries and other baked goods — covering the flow from raw material inspection through preparation, baking, packaging and dispatch. I'm committed to continuous improvement and to keeping food safety standards high at every step of production and storage.
